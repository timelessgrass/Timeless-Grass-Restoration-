FAQs

Turf cleaning questions, answered

How professional cleaning works, what makes Timeless different, and how to protect your turf.

1Why not just use a DIY turf spray?

Sprays treat the surface. The smell comes back because urine and bacteria stay trapped in the infill and backing. We extract them with professional equipment, then treat what's left so the odor doesn't return.

2What makes your process different?

We install turf for a living, so we clean it the way it was built. That means extraction instead of a perfume spray, checking seams and drainage, restoring infill and lifting matted blades. Then we finish it blown clean.

3How long does a turf cleaning take?

Most residential yards are done in a single visit, usually a few hours on site. We typically schedule this week or next.

4How much does turf cleaning cost?

We price by square footage and condition, never by the hour. For yards up to 500 sq ft, an Essential Clean is $199 and a Premium Restoration is $299. TIMELESS CARE memberships start at $89/month, and there's a $199 minimum per visit. See the full price table, or call 303-349-2368 for a free quote.

5When can kids and pets use the turf again?

As soon as it's dry, which is usually the same day. We use products that are safe for turf, kids and pets.

6Can you restore my putting green?

Yes, putting greens are our specialty. We top-dress, brush and roll to bring back speed and a true roll, repair seams and cups, and clean up the edges and fringe.

7Do you work with HOAs, daycares and commercial properties?

Yes. We work on homes, HOA common areas, pet daycares, play areas, sports fields and commercial properties. Ask us about recurring maintenance for high-traffic turf.

8Is it cheaper to clean turf or replace it?

Cleaning and restoring is almost always the smarter first move. A deep clean, fresh infill and a blade lift can add years of life. If your turf is truly past saving, we'll tell you, and we can install new.

9Do you install new turf too?

Yes. TIMELESS Grass & Greens installs putting greens, artificial lawns, pet turf, commercial and sports turf, all with American-made turf.
