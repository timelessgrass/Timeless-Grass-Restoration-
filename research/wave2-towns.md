## Garden City (CDP, Horry + Georgetown counties; ZIP 29576; oceanfront)
10,235 pop (2020), median age 61.5; 5.45 sq mi south of Surfside to the Murrells Inlet peninsula; 668-ft pier; ~54.6 in rain. Hazel (1954) left two habitable houses; Hugo (1989) destroyed 43% of beachfront structures. Water: GSWSA (Horry side); Georgetown County W&SD Garden City Point buys GSWSA water. Housing: beach houses/condos, weekly rentals dominant; Inlet Harbour (gated), Oceanside Village, Salters Cove (pool/tennis). Pets: Surfside Bark Park and A Dog's Way Inn park nearest; Bark! Pet Resort (11670 Frontage Rd). Golf: Indian Wells Golf Club, Tupelo Bay Golf Center. Unincorporated (Horry County police/fire); no STR permit ordinance found.

## Loris (city, inc. 1957; Horry; ZIP 29569; inland ~20 mi)
2,449 pop (2020); 4.6 sq mi at US 701/SC 9; railroad town founded 1887; Bog-Off Festival. Water: City of Loris buys from GSWSA (MB surface plant + Bull Creek); no hardness. Housing: rural small town; Woodland Valley on Diamondback Golf Club. Pets: Loris Nature Park (county passive park with fenced dog park, disc golf, trails; address not found); Morlen Kennels (3121 Green Sea Rd S); K9 Pandemonium; Pampered Paws. Golf: Diamondback Golf Club at Woodland Valley. City stormwater ordinance not found; Horry Ch. 17.7 outside limits.

## Burgess (unincorporated Horry; ZIPs 29576/29588; inland/ICW side ~3 mi)
~28 sq mi bounded by Waccamaw River (W), US 17 Bypass (E), Georgetown line (S), SC 31/707 (N); ~3,000 pop (1990) → ~25,000 (2010) → 31,037 (2018–19); Burgess Community Center 10299 Hwy 707. Water: GSWSA. Housing: rural → suburban; TPC of Myrtle Beach community, Blackmoor, Laurel Woods; Cypress River Plantation (gated, ICW, 500+ lots, boat landing); Greater Burgess Community Association counts 60 neighborhoods; a 2,000-home development proposed. Pets: no dog park; Dog Boarding at the Beach (401 Tyner St) and Bark! Pet Resort in the 707/Bypass corridor. Golf: TPC Myrtle Beach, Blackmoor.

## Calabash, NC (town; Brunswick; ZIP 28467; river/ICW, ~2 mi to Sunset Beach strand)
2,011 pop (2020), median age 56.4, 32.9% 65+; 4.87 sq mi; tidal Calabash River to Little River SC; "Seafood Capital of the World." Water: Brunswick County system (hardness 28.07). Housing: retiree golf communities — Brunswick Plantation (2,000 ac, 27 holes, 55+), Crow Creek (gated, private course), Meadowlands; adjacent Carolina Shores (The Farm at Brunswick, Calabash Lakes, Beacon Townes, Lighthouse Cove). Pets: VCA Seaside Animal Hospital boarding/daycare; no town dog park (nearest OIB Park). Golf: Brunswick Plantation, Crow Creek, Meadowlands, Carolina Shores GC, Farmstead.

## Sunset Beach, NC (town inc. 1963; Brunswick; ZIP 28468; oceanfront island + mainland)
4,175 pop (2020), median age 68, 60.1% 65+; 7.88 sq mi, ~⅓ barrier island; island ~1,200 homes mostly vacation rentals, ~100 full-time residents; 2010 arc bridge; EF3 tornado Feb 2021. Water: Brunswick County system. Housing: island rentals; mainland Sea Trail Plantation (2,000+ ac; Rees Jones/Willard Byrd/Dan Maples courses), Thistle Golf Estates (27 holes), Sandpiper Bay; Ocean Ridge Plantation nearby. STR: council approved GovOS registration/compliance contract Oct 2, 2023 (est. 637 STRs, ~200 then compliant). Beach dog rule above. Golf: Sea Trail (54 holes), Thistle, Sandpiper Bay, Oyster Bay, The Pearl.

## Ocean Isle Beach, NC (town inc. 1959; Brunswick; ZIP 28469; barrier island)
867 pop (2020); island 5 mi Tubbs Inlet to Shallotte Inlet; 4.72 sq mi incl. mainland; retirees/second homes. Water: Town of OIB on the island (Brunswick County wholesale; Northwest WTP, Cape Fear River). Housing: beach houses/condos, seasonal rentals; mainland Ocean Ridge Plantation (gated, since early 1990s, planned 3,000 homes; Big Cats courses Lion's Paw, Panther's Run, Tiger's Eye, Leopard's Chase; private beach club on Sunset Beach), Brick Landing Plantation, The Retreat, Sunset Ridge, Bent Tree, Chatham Glenn. Pets: Ocean Isle Beach Park dog park, 6483 Old Georgetown Rd SW (two fenced areas); Ruffin It Doggy Daycare. Accommodations tax >$5.3M FY2023-24 (secondary). Town prohibits depositing material in drainage channels.

## Shallotte, NC (town inc. 1899; Brunswick; ZIP 28470; inland river town ~5–6 mi)

Housing: Rivers Edge Golf Club & Plantation (gated; Arnold Palmer Signature 18; oceanfront beach club, pools, pickleball), Brierwood Estates (Brierwood Golf Club). Pets: Shallotte Township District Park fenced dog park, 5550 Main St; Tiki Paws Doggie Daycare & Boarding, 1360 Whiteville Rd. Golf: Rivers Edge, Brierwood.
