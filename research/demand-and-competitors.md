## What this changes
1. Myrtle Beach town page is the flagship money page; the only page to beat is one franchise service page.
2. Pet-odor cluster and the mold/algae page are the best informational targets (Reddit-heavy or low-DR SERPs).
3. Public pricing is a differentiator; keep the table on /pricing/ and the cost guide.
4. Storm/sand/pine-needle pages: near-zero volume. Kept because they are substantive and they feed the town pages and the seasonal service; do not expand that cluster further.
5. Product-intent terms (cleaner, deodorizer, power broom) are comparison-guide material, not service pages.
